1. Information the Activity handles

When you use the Activity, it may process:

  • Discord account information: The server verifies a Discord access token with Discord to obtain the user identity and display name needed to seat and identify players.
  • Gameplay information: Table membership, game state, scores, player names, and recent winners/results may be processed to run and display multiplayer games.
  • Guest information: When the Activity is opened outside Discord, it supports guest tables and may process a guest identifier and the name you enter.
  • Technical information: The hosting provider may collect technical data such as request logs or connection information as part of operating and securing its services. Check your host’s own privacy information for details of its practices.

The project files indicate that recent game results can be saved to a JSON file. They do not establish how long that file is retained in your deployed setup; set and document a retention period that reflects your actual configuration.

2. How information is used

We use information to:

  • Authenticate Discord users and connect them to a game seat.
  • Operate multiplayer tables, synchronize gameplay, and display scores or results.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Activity.
  • Respond to user requests or address misuse.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by:

  • Discord, when the Activity is used within Discord, under Discord’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • FadeHost or another hosting provider you choose, to host and deliver the Activity.
  • Other providers only if you add them and they are needed to operate the Activity.

We may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the Activity, its users, or others.
